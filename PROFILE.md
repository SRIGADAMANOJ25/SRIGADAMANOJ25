## Why NEXTbite360?

The food & beverage industry has not seen significant innovation in R&D workflows. Most companies still rely on:
- Manual formulation tracking
- Disconnected data silos
- High trial-and-error cycles
- Inefficient lab-to-production handoffs

**NEXTbite360 solves this** by creating a unified platform that connects market insights → concepts → formulations → lab experiments → production data, enabling teams to:
- ✅ Reduce time-to-market by 30-50%
- ✅ Minimize failed batches and waste
- ✅ Improve collaboration across teams
- ✅ Build institutional knowledge systematically
