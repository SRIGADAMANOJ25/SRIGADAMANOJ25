Today, I'm building **NEXTbite360** at **Mirai Sozo Labs**—bringing that experience into tools that help product teams connect what they know, decide what to test, and learn from the results.

## Building NEXTbite360

**An AI-powered product development workspace—from research and ideas to formulations, experiments, and learning.**

Product development needs more than a promising concept or a generated recipe. It needs evidence, practical constraints, well-designed trials, and a record of why decisions were made.

I'm building NEXTbite360 to connect those pieces:

```text
Research → Concept → Formulation → Experiment → Results → Learning
                         ↑                                  │
                         └──────────────────────────────────┘
```

| Development focus | What I'm working toward |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Research & ideation** | Connect market signals, scientific literature, and customer briefs to evidence-backed product opportunities. |
| **Formulation & experimentation** | Bring ingredient choices, development constraints, formulation versions, and lab plans into a connected workflow. |
| **Results & project knowledge** | Keep measurements, sources, decisions, and learnings connected across development cycles. |
| **AI-assisted collaboration** | Help teams investigate questions, compare options, and prepare next steps while keeping consequential decisions under human control. |

My domain expertise is rooted in food and beverage. The broader vision is to support evidence-led product development across industries, with research and workflows adapted to each domain and market.

**The goal: fewer blind trials, clearer decisions, and less knowledge lost between projects.**

## How I approach AI in R&D

AI should help teams ask better questions, find stronger evidence, and design more informative experiments—not turn uncertainty into false confidence.

I care about **traceable sources, explicit assumptions, reproducible analysis, and human review**. A generated formulation is a starting point for evaluation, not proof that a product works.
